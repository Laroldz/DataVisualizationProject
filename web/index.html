Percentages compare the mix of crashes, so big and small places can be read side by side. Cities and roads have no population in FARS, so they get counts and shares but no per-100k rate. Roads use crashes with map locations (2001 on).

Before / after a law

See how crashes changed after a state traffic law (or any date you enter) took effect, next to the same change in a comparison group, so national trends aren't mistaken for the law's effect.

Indexed trend

Before vs after

This compares trends; it can't prove the law caused the change, since other things (enforcement, the economy, other laws) can shift at the same time. State law years come from the Correlates of State Policy Project and are only known to the year, so the years when a law took effect are left out; custom events use the exact date, with 12-month periods on each side. Its records end around 2010–2016, so newer laws aren't listed; use a custom event for those.
